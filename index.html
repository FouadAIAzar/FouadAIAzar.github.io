Bio

I am a Research Engineer and bioinformatician in multi-omics at the Public Health Research Center (PHRC), NYU Abu Dhabi.

Research:
I develop and maintain the laboratory information management system for participant visits, biospecimen tracking and biobank inventory, and I analyse serum metabolomics data from the UAE Healthy Future Study to investigate metabolic syndrome and polycystic ovary syndrome.

Approaches:
I build reproducible R workflows for differential abundance, metabolite ratios and network analysis, and I work across machine learning, multivariate statistics, and optical, fluorescence and NMR spectroscopy. I also coordinate assay planning and clinical data linkage with cohort investigators.

Biosketch:
I studied Biomedical Engineering at FH Aachen University of Applied Sciences in Jülich, Germany (B.Eng., M.Sc.). I coordinated the FLURINE multimodal spectroscopy project at the Institute of Bioengineering, worked as a self-employed biomedical engineer and full-stack developer, and curated multi-omics datasets as a bioinformatician at Prepaire before joining PHRC in 2025.
