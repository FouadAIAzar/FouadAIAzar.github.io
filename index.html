Bio

Research: I am a biomedical engineer and bioinformatician working on multi-omics databases, machine learning and optical spectroscopy. At NYU Abu Dhabi's Public Health Research Center I lead development of OmiχLIMS, the laboratory information management system behind the centre's sample and study data.

Approach: My work sits between the lab and the data: building the databases, pipelines and analysis tooling that take genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic and metabolomic measurements from collection through to integrated, analysable datasets.

Background: I trained in biomedical engineering at FH Aachen, Germany (BEng, MSc), with a focus on medical physics, signal processing and bioinformatics. Before joining PHRC I curated multi-omic databases as a bioinformatician at Prepaire, and coordinated multi-modal spectroscopy and machine-learning work for urinalysis at the Institute of Bioengineering.
